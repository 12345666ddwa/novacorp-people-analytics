Statistical appendix · Decile regression

Better managers mean fewer exits. The slope is real.

Each point is one decile of 12,590 employees, ranked by manager effectiveness. The regression line has R² = 0.60, p = 0.008.

0.52 pp lower attrition per 1 point of manager effectiveness.
0.60 R². The decile spread is mostly explained by this line, not noise.
0.008 p-value. The chance this slope is noise sits under 1 in 100.

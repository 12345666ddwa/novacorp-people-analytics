05 · Why People Leave

6 in 10 exits could be fixed by NovaCorp itself

1,133 people left voluntarily. 688 of those exits were push, caused by something inside NovaCorp. Only 445 were pulled away by outside offers.

60.7% of voluntary exits are push. The person left because of NovaCorp, not the market.
688 people left for internal reasons. 445 left for external offers.
1,133 voluntary exits in total. Six in ten were fixable from the inside.
RecommendationFix manager trust and recognition first, before chasing market pay.

Exit rates for promotion eligible staff against everyone else, by level. The gap stays tiny at every level.

09 · The HiPo Paradox

High potentials leave 50% more often

The HiPo label should mean we fight harder to keep them. The data says the opposite is happening.

12.5% of high potentials left. 50% more than everyone else.
8.1% of everyone else left in the same window.
29% of HiPos are marked promotion eligible. The promise is not kept.
RecommendationGive HiPos a visible path or stop calling them high potential.

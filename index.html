5.3 【关键方法学陷阱】Entity_B × 短 Tenure 的结构假象5.3 Critical Methodological Trap: Entity_B × Short Tenure Artifact

⚠ 重要警告：XGBoost 表面最强特征 entityB_x_earlytenure（importance=0.322）和交互表"Entity_B × <12月 = 79.4% 离职（181/228）"看似惊人，但经深入数据核查，这是收购结构假象，绝非真实的"新员工离职率"。

三个原因：

  1. Entity_B 全部 1,884 名员工 hire 年均为 2024 年 - - 因此 Entity_B 的离职者 tenure 定义上就全部 <24 月（283/283=100%）。"短 tenure 离职"是收购时间轴的固有算术属性。
  2. 对比：NovaCorp-Origin 成熟 tenure 结构下，<12 月离职率仅 14.0%（31/221），远非 100%。
  3. 正确解读：Entity_B 高离职 = 收购整合问题（文化融合、管理层变更、认可体系迁移压力），而非"雇人雇错了"。把 entityB×earlytenure 当作可干预驱动会严重误导预算投向。

⚠ Critical Warning: XGBoost's top feature entityB_x_earlytenure (importance=0.322) and the interaction table showing "Entity_B × <12m = 79.4% turnover (181/228)" appear dramatic. However, after deep data verification, this is an acquisition-structure artifact, not a real "new hire turnover rate."

Three reasons:

  1. All 1,884 Entity_B employees have hire year 2024 - so every Entity_B leaver has tenure <24 months by definition (283/283=100%).
  2. NovaCorp-Origin <12m turnover is only 14.0% (31/221), far from 100%.
  3. Correct interpretation: Entity_B high turnover = acquisition integration problems (cultural fusion, management changes, recognition system migration). Treating entityB×earlytenure as an addressable driver would severely misdirect budget allocation.

11. 局限性与方法论反思11. Limitations & Methodology Reflections

  1. 因果与相关的边界：尽管采用了滞后设计来增强因果推断，HR 观察数据本质上无法完全排除未观测变量的混淆。报告的结论应理解为"在现有数据下哪些因素表现出最强的时间先后关联和预测能力"，而非严格意义上的"因果效应"。
  2. Entity_B 结构假象的潜在残留：虽然识别并剥离了 entityB×earlytenure 的收购算术假象，但 Entity_B 相关其他特征可能仍携带未完全分离的结构效应。Entity_B 的离职驱动解读需结合定性访谈三角验证。
  3. engagement 非响应者的信息不对称：10,264 条非响应记录被标记为特征 - - 未参与调查本身就是敬业度低的信号，但也意味着无法获知这些员工对 8 个维度的真实评分。
  4. 2025-H2 绩效数据缺失：距数据截止日期最近的评估周期缺失，削弱了"最近绩效→离职"路径的可靠性。
  5. 离职面谈原因可信度：Case Brief 明确指出 40-60% 的离职面谈原因不反映真实原因。本报告将 stated_exit_reason 作为参考信号而非核心变量。
  6. 模型 AUC 的诚实解读：测试集 AUC 的 0.915 和 0.884 很大程度来自收购结构提供的"容易信号"。当剥离收购相关特征后，仅靠软性驱动因子的预测能力会下降 - - 但这恰恰是"真正可干预"的部分。高分 AUC 不应被误读为"模型已完美解释离职"。
  7. ROI 测算的不确定性：五杠杆 ROI 基于三情景方法，涉及多环节假设叠加。建议将 ROI 视为优先级排序的相对标尺，而非财务审计的精确预估。
  1. Causality and correlation boundary: Despite the lagged design strengthening causal inference, HR observational data cannot fully exclude confounding from unobserved variables. Conclusions should be understood as "which factors show the strongest time-sequenced association and predictive power" - not strict "causal effects."
  2. Residual Entity_B structural artifact: Although the entityB×earlytenure acquisition arithmetic artifact was removed, other Entity_B features may still carry incompletely separated structural effects. Entity_B driver interpretation requires triangulation with qualitative interviews.
  3. Engagement non-responder information asymmetry: 10,264 non-response records are marked as a feature - non-participation signals low engagement, but true scores on 8 dimensions remain unknown.
  4. Missing 2025-H2 performance data: The absence of the review cycle closest to the data cutoff date weakens the "recent performance → turnover" prediction pathway.
  5. Exit interview reason reliability: 40-60% of exit interview reasons do not reflect true reasons (per Case Brief). This report uses stated_exit_reason as a reference signal, not a core variable.
  6. Honest AUC interpretation: Test AUCs of 0.915 and 0.884 are heavily driven by acquisition-structure "easy signals." When acquisition features are removed, predictive power from soft drivers would decrease - but these are precisely the "truly actionable" portion.
  7. ROI estimation uncertainty: Five-lever ROI estimates involve multi-step assumption stacking. ROI should be treated as a relative priority-ranking scale, not precise financial audit estimates.
